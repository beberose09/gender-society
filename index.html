Milestones in gender equality

Progress has come in steps, often after long campaigns by ordinary people.

  1. 1893

    New Zealand becomes the first self-governing country to give women the right to vote in national elections.

  2. 1948

    The Universal Declaration of Human Rights affirms equal rights for everyone, regardless of sex.

  3. 1979

    The UN adopts CEDAW, a treaty that commits countries to ending discrimination against women.

  4. 1995

    The Beijing Declaration and Platform for Action sets a global agenda for women's rights.

  5. 2006

    The Yogyakarta Principles apply international human rights law to sexual orientation and gender identity.

  6. 2015

    Gender equality becomes Goal 5 of the UN Sustainable Development Goals.

Key terms

Shared vocabulary makes discussion clearer. Select a term to read its meaning.

Sex

A classification, usually assigned at birth, based on biological characteristics such as chromosomes, hormones and anatomy.

Gender

The social roles, behaviours and identities that a society associates with being a woman, a man or another gender.

Gender identity

A person's own sense of their gender, which may or may not match the sex they were assigned at birth.

Gender roles

Shared expectations about how people of a given gender should act, dress and spend their time.

Patriarchy

A social system in which men hold most positions of power, authority and property.

Intersectionality

The idea, introduced by Kimberlé Crenshaw, that gender combines with race, class, disability and other factors to shape a person's experience.

Gender mainstreaming

Building attention to gender into every policy, budget and programme, instead of treating it as a separate issue.

Where to read next

The Second Sex

Simone de Beauvoir, 1949

A foundational study of how women are defined in relation to men.

Gender Trouble

Judith Butler, 1990

Argues that gender is something people perform repeatedly, not a fixed trait.

Invisible Women

Caroline Criado Perez, 2019

Shows how data that ignores women leads to products and policies that fail them.

Ain't I a Woman?

bell hooks, 1981

Examines the overlap of race, class and gender in Black women's lives.
